Capstone Proposal

My topic is about showing the difference between the everyday person compared to the general population and ultra rich. The project is an interactive story where users follow a character through a regular day.It will go through commuting, eating, working, and resting, while comparing it to an average person and the super wealthy. The audience should just be generally everyone, since they should fit in the shoes of my everyday person.
This will definitely use data visualization a lot to see comparisons. It will help show differences in things like time, cost, or quality of life.
It will be a scrollable story with data(charts, graphs)to show comparisons. One thing I am not too sure about is how to end the narrative.
