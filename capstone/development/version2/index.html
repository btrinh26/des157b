Climate Change in 2025

CO₂ levels hit 400ppm, which is the highest in 3 million years.
The hottest 8 years on record happened after 2015.

Why did this happen?

For more than a century, our energy, industry, and transportation systems relied on fossil fuel, which pump greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. We ignored the signs until they overwhelmed us.

What will happen if this continues?

Sea levels will rise a lot. heatwaves will kill thousands, and ecosystems will collapse. Our inaction for climate change will have consequences beyond for thousands of years.

What my world did to change this

We transitioned to renewables, built cleaner infrastructure, and rewrote laws for to better climate change. It wasn’t easy, but it worked. The world began to heal.
